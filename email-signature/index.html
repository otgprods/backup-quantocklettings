Your new email signature

Pick your name, check your details, then copy the signature and paste it into Outlook using the steps for your device.

On a phone? Pick your name, then scroll down to iPhone and use Copy phone signature. The full signature is for computers and comes out with lines and cut-off text in the phone apps.

1. Check your details

Leave a box empty and that line is left out of the signature. Changes show straight away below.

2. Copy the signature

Copied. Now paste it into Outlook.

If the button does nothing, click just above “Kind regards”, drag down to the end of the small print to highlight the whole signature, then press Ctrl + C (Windows) or Cmd + C (Mac).

3. Paste it into Outlook

Open this page on the computer you are setting up, copy the signature, then follow the steps for that version of Outlook. Each computer needs doing once.

Outlook on a Windows PC (the classic version)
  1. Open Outlook and click File, then Options, then Mail, then the Signatures… button.
  2. Click New, type the name QL 2026 and click OK.
  3. Click in the big empty box under Edit signature and press Ctrl + V.
  4. On the right, in the E-mail account box, choose office@quantocklettings.co.uk. Then set New messages and Replies/forwards to QL 2026.
  5. Click OK, then OK again.

If you had an old signature, select it in the list and click Delete once the new one is working, so it can’t be picked by accident.

New Outlook for Windows, or Outlook in a web browser
  1. Click the cog (Settings) at the top right.
  2. Go to Account, then Signatures. If you have more than one email account, pick office@quantocklettings.co.uk at the top.
  3. Click + New signature, name it QL 2026, click in the box and press Ctrl + V.
  4. Click Save.
  5. Under Choose default signatures, pick QL 2026 for both For new messages and For replies/forwards, then click Save.
Outlook on a Mac
  1. Open Outlook. In the menu bar at the top of the screen click Outlook, then Settings, then Signatures.
  2. Click the + button, name it QL 2026, click in the box and press Cmd + V.
  3. Save it, then under Choose default signature pick office@quantocklettings.co.uk, and choose QL 2026 for New messages and Replies/forwards.
iPhone (Outlook app or Apple Mail)

Phones keep their own signature, separate from the computer. On the phone, only use this shorter version. Pasting the full signature into the Outlook app gives it lines and cuts off the small print.

Copied.

Outlook app on iPhone

  1. Open this page on the iPhone, pick your name at the top, then tap Copy phone signature.
  2. Open the Outlook app, tap your picture at the top left, then the cog at the bottom left.
  3. Tap Signature, delete what is there, then press and hold in the box and tap Paste. Tap back to save.

Apple Mail on iPhone

  1. Copy the phone signature as above.
  2. Open Settings, then Apps, then Mail, then Signature.
  3. Delete what is there, press and hold in the box and tap Paste, then go back.

4. Check it

  1. Start a new email in Outlook. The signature should appear on its own, with the logo.
  2. Send one test email to Marcus so he can check it on his side.

If Outlook shows a small box where the logo should be, click Download pictures at the top of the email, or check the computer is online. The pictures load from the Quantock Lettings website.
